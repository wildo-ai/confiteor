# CONFITEOR — the clip prompt book (Grok Imagine 1.5, 1080p)

**Paul reads this before anything is spent.** 22 clips (C72 revised after review round 1: the candle now goes out on the word), about 112 s, about **$28** at $0.25/s for one
take each; budget a second take on a third of them, so about $40.

How to read an entry: `id · storyboard shots · song time of the shot · length generated`. The first
frame is the plate; times inside a prompt are seconds from the clip's first frame. Grok cannot hear
the song, so a beat is placed two ways: the prompt puts the action at a stated second, and the edit
then slides the clip so that frame lands exactly on the song's hit (measured from the clip's motion,
not assumed). Nobody's mouth forms words in any clip. All type is drawn by the engine.

Song facts used below (take v2-44, `song/hits.json`): 123 BPM, a bar is 1.95 s; in the verses the
press **clank** falls every two bars (22.67, 26.61, 30.53, 34.48, 38.41, 42.34, 46.27 s); in the
choruses each shouted **CULPA** is the strike (60.80, 64.74, 70.62, 115.86, 119.80, 125.68 s).

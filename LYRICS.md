# CONFITEOR (BE HONEST) — lyrics v1 and their sources

The sung lyric is in `song/full-v1.plan.json`. This file is what each factual line rests on. A line
that names a fact either has a source here, or it does not ship.

## The lyric

```
[Intro]          Confiteor...
                 I wrote... tests.            — Be honest.
                 Okay. I ran it. Once.        — Be honest.
                 I pushed it straight to prod.
                 Thank you.

[Verse 1]        Mainz, fourteen ninety-two. Three a.m. One candle.
                 The abbot writes against the press: too fast, too much to handle.
                 A copy made by hand will last a thousand years.
                 A monk who copies, prays. Wipe the ink away. Wipe the tears.
                 He writes it all by hand, with love, against the black machine,
                 then sends it to the printer. Mainz, fourteen ninety-four. Clean.

[Pre]            Be honest. Be honest.
[Chorus 1]       Who wrote the tests?                     MEA CULPA!
                 Who read the code?                       MEA CULPA!
                 Who held a candle to every line?         MEA CULPA!
                 Be honest...

[Verse 2]        Twenty-five percent. Then thirty. Then a half.
                 Then three quarters of the new code. Go ahead and laugh.
                 You still read it line by line, a candle and a quill,
                 green ticks like a rosary, and you count them still.
                 Looks good to me. Amen. Works on my machine. Amen.
                 Ship it on a Friday, then confess it all again.

[Pre]            Be honest. Be honest.
[Chorus 2]       Who wrote the tests?                     MEA CULPA!
                 Who approved the merge?                  MEA CULPA!
                 Who swore it worked on every machine?    MEA CULPA!
                 Be honest...

[Bridge]         It was never about the tests.
                 It was the candle you were holding,
                 in a room you called the world.
                 Outside, the world is printing.
                 Look up.

[Break]          Copied by hand.   DEPRECATED.
                 Checked by eye.   DEPRECATED.
                 Read every line.  DEPRECATED.
                 And you?

[Final chorus]   Be honest: who wrote the tests?                 I DID!
                 Be honest: who read the code?                   I DID!
                 Be honest: who's still holding the candle?
                 (the Scribe, alone)  I did.
                 (the Machine)        Thank you.

[Outro]          Confiteor...
```

Chorus 2's second line was "Who read the diff?" in v1 and was changed to "Who approved the merge?" in v2: every take sang "diff" so it was heard as "dove" or "tip".

Voices: **the Scribe** (a tired young man: the intro and his last "I did"); **the Machine** (a calm,
close female voice: every verse, the bridge, both "Thank you"s); **the choir** (male, Gregorian:
"Be honest" in the intro); **the crowd** (stacked male voices: MEA CULPA, DEPRECATED, and in the
flip, I DID: the machine's copies answering).

| Drop after chorus 1 (no lyric, on screen) | "European printing presses c. 1600 could produce between 1,500 and 3,600 impressions per workday" | Wikipedia, *Printing press*, citing Wolf 1974, pp. 67f. The caption says **c. 1600**: it is not a 1494 figure |
| Verse 2 | 75% of new code, "AI-generated and approved by engineers" (Cloud Next, Apr 2026) | [Google blog, Cloud Next 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/cloud-next-2026-sundar-pichai/) · [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91531519/google-ceo-says-75-of-the-companys-code-is-ai-generated) |

On screen, the curve names Google and the date of each point, and says "new code", never "all code".
Pichai's own qualifier is that engineers still review and approve every line. Verse 2's "you still read
it line by line" is that qualifier, sung back.
